CASE STUDY: CIVICOS · PRODUCT PERSPECTIVE

CivicOS aggregates fragmented municipal information, service requests, and geospatial infrastructure data into an intuitive single-pane-of-glass civic operating system.

TARGET AUDIENCE: Urban citizens, civic leaders, community organizers, and municipal department heads navigating civic services.
THE PROBLEM BEING SOLVED

Municipal portals are notoriously siloed, antiquated, and difficult to navigate. Citizens lack visibility into local infrastructure issues, leading to duplicated reports and slow resolution cycles.

THE ENGINEERED SOLUTION

A unified digital command center featuring interactive map exploration, AI-assisted natural language issue reporting, and public resolution dashboards.

KEY DELIVERED FEATURES:
Interactive Geospatial Map with live status markers
AI-powered natural language query and reporting interface
Real-time municipal telemetry and analytics dashboards
Transparent issue tracking pipeline from report to resolution
Responsive design accessible on all desktop and mobile devices
