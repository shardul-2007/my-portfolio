CivicOS

AI Municipal Intelligence Platform

CivicOS is an AI-powered municipal intelligence platform designed to make civic information, workflows and public-service interactions more accessible, structured and intelligent. It brings together maps, data analytics and an intelligent interface to connect citizens with civic services.

  • Interactive Maps
  • Data Analytics
  • AI Intelligence Layer
  • Civic Services Interface
React Vite Leaflet Recharts AI Integration REST APIs
Live Demo

Problem

Civic information is often fragmented and inaccessible. Public services lack a unified, intelligent interface — leaving citizens without clear ways to access or engage with municipal resources.

Solution

CivicOS provides a structured, AI-augmented interface that consolidates civic data, maps infrastructure and presents public-service information in an accessible, human-readable form.

Impact

CivicOS demonstrates how AI and modern web technology can reduce friction between citizens and public services — establishing a foundation for intelligent municipal tooling.
