CivicOS

AI Municipal Intelligence Platform

CivicOS is an AI-powered municipal intelligence platform designed to make civic information, workflows and public-service interactions more accessible, structured and intelligent. It brings together maps, data analytics and an intelligent interface to connect citizens with civic services.

Interactive Maps
Data Analytics
AI Intelligence Layer
Civic Services
React Vite Leaflet Recharts AI Integration REST APIs
Live Demo

Problem

Civic information is often fragmented, difficult to navigate and inaccessible to ordinary citizens. Public services lack a unified, intelligent interface — leaving people without clear ways to understand, access or engage with municipal resources and processes.

Solution

CivicOS addresses this by providing a structured, AI-augmented interface that consolidates civic data, maps infrastructure and presents public-service information in an accessible, human-readable form.

CivicOS demonstrates how AI and modern web technology can make civic systems more human — reducing friction between citizens and public services, and establishing a foundation for intelligent municipal tooling.
